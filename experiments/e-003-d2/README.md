# E-003-D2


E-003-D2 tested a harder event-bound partial-addressing mechanism while keeping numerical capacity comparable between BIND and SHAM conditions.


The implementation and analysis were frozen and audited before the final result package. Technical gates passed, but the mechanism was rejected: private inference and intrinsic-valence coupling remained observable, while robust reconstruction/newcomer advantages over false binding and visible-sequence controls did not.


## Archival packages


The large immutable packages are prepared outside Git history as release/Zenodo assets:


- `Schwarmwelt – E003-D2 – Projektions- und Unabhängigkeitsaudit.zip`
- `Schwarmwelt – E003-D2 – Vor-Freeze-Review-Paket.zip`
- `Schwarmwelt – E003-D2 – Implementierungs-Freeze.zip`
- `Schwarmwelt – E003-D2 – vollständiges Ergebnispaket.zip`


These packages contain nested historical source archives. During publication preparation they were recursively rescanned to nesting depth 8; no credential candidates, e-mail addresses, user-specific absolute paths or suspicious credential filenames were detected.


The negative E-003-D2 result motivated E-004-PRG to introduce a cheaper Stage-A admission gate before spending additional core-run budget.
